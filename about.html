Our Philosophy

Clothes that feel effortless.

सहज और स्वाभाविक परिधान।

Urban Threads began with a simple idea: good clothes should feel effortless. We choose fabrics that last, cut them to fit cleanly, and leave out the noise.

अर्बन थ्रेड्स की शुरुआत एक सहज विचार के साथ हुई: अच्छे कपड़े पहनने में स्वाभाविक और आरामदायक होने चाहिए। हम ऐसे कपड़े चुनते हैं जो टिकाऊ हों, सही कट में हों, और दिखावे से दूर हों।

Fabric First

We source high-grade Egyptian Giza cotton, natural European linen blends, and double-mercerized yarns. In Mumbai's humid atmosphere, breathability and structural resilience are non-negotiable.

Fit That Works

Every seam, yoke, and armhole is calibrated specifically for Indian body frames. We balance structured shoulders with relaxed mobility so you never feel restricted at work or over dinner.

Nothing In Excess

No loud logos, no artificial discount countdowns, and no disposable fast-fashion cycles. Only refined staples that you will reach for season after season.

Experience the fit in Bandra

Visit our physical showroom on Linking Road, Bandra West. Touch the cotton weaves, compare collar rolls, and meet our in-store master tailor.
